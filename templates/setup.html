    Recommended body-camera POV

    Prioritize a clear seated upper-body view

    Place the camera almost directly in front of the participant. The head, shoulders, elbows, hands, and hips must stay visible. Knees and feet improve full-body analysis but are optional in the default seated-monitoring profile.

    Distance
    About 1.2–2.0 m
    Height
    Waist to chest level
    Angle
    Front view; 0–15° from center
    Tilt
    Level or slightly downward

    Face view

    • Camera is at eye level
    • Both eyes and full face are clear
    • Participant sits about 35–45 cm away

    Body view checklist

    • Required: head, shoulders, elbows, hands, and hips stay visible
    • Recommended: include knees and feet when room space permits
    • Leave space beside both arms and avoid a strong side or low-floor angle
